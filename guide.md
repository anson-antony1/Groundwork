## Important Reminders About DevPost:

- Make sure everyone on your team has joined & you all are in a team
- Make sure to opt-in to any challenges or additional track
- Everyone qualifies for the General Track (Overall Prize)
- Recorded Video Demos are not required
    - If you have the time, we would recommend recording one for future reference.

## Project Expo

The **Project Expo** is where you will showcase your submitted project to the judges. The Expo is set up like a science fair, where judges will come to you and view your project. Judges will spend *around 2-3 minutes* viewing your project and learning about your work. Various judges will come by, we expect each team to viewed by at least 3 judges; however, this could be more (dependent on how many challenges you have opted-in).

Project Expo occurs in Reitz Union’s Grand and Rion Ballrooms. All hackers will be there from **10 AM - 12 PM**. Our team will send out an announcement letting all hackers know that they can come set up for the expo and their table number. Make sure to bring any necessary materials to present your project to judges.

Please do **not** switch tables or numbers, let our team know if you need to switch tables (especially if you need access to outlets to present your project).
